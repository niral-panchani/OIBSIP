About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he rose from humble beginnings to become one of the most respected leaders in the country.

He played an important role in India's space and missile programmes. His work with ISRO and DRDO contributed to India's scientific and defence capabilities. He became widely known as the "Missile Man of India."

Beyond science and politics, Dr. Kalam was a passionate teacher who encouraged young people to dream big and work hard. His simplicity, dedication and belief in education continue to inspire students across the country.

Key Achievements

  • 1931 Born on October 15 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  • 1960 Began his career at DRDO after graduating in aerospace engineering.
  • 1969 Joined ISRO and contributed to India's space programme.
  • 1980 Played a key role in the successful launch of the SLV-3 satellite launch vehicle.
  • 1997 Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.
  • 2002–2007 Served as the 11th President of India.
